🏥 Diabetes Patient Data Analysis

Analysing 768 patient records to find key insights about diabetes

By Priyanka Palai | BCA Data Science | Aditya Degree College

About the Project

This project analyses a real-world diabetes dataset containing medical records of 768 patients. The dataset includes health parameters like Glucose levels, Blood Pressure, BMI, Age, and Diabetes Outcome.

The goal is to identify key patterns and trends that indicate diabetes risk using Python, Excel, and Tableau.

Key Findings

  • 35% of patients in the dataset are diabetic — 1 in every 3 patients has diabetes
  • Diabetic patients have significantly higher average glucose (141.26) compared to non-diabetic patients (109.98)
  • Patients below 25 years have very low diabetes occurrence
  • Diabetes risk increases significantly after age 35
  • Peak diabetic cases are found in the 40-60 age group
  • High glucose level is the strongest indicator of diabetes

Conclusion

This analysis reveals that patients with high glucose levels and older age groups are at significantly higher risk of diabetes. Early screening for people above 35 with high glucose levels is strongly recommended. Regular monitoring of glucose, BMI, and blood pressure can help in early detection and prevention of diabetes.
